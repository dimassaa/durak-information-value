The search arms are weaker than the heuristic

Both search arms are markedly WEAKER than the plain GREEDY heuristic: on this dataset each loses essentially every game to it. That is a finding about the policies -- the search is depth-limited and its evaluation is misweighted -- and not about the engine or about information. Report 1's price of information is measured between the two arms and is unaffected, because a weakness shared by both cancels out of that comparison.

Two rates, two different questions

Two different rates are on this page and they answer different questions. A MARGINAL rate is measured against the whole field, so it moves when a strategy joins or leaves the tournament and cannot be compared across studies with different strategy palettes. The OPPONENT-INDEPENDENT rate is measured against RANDOM alone, so its opponent is fixed and it IS comparable across studies. Quote that one.
